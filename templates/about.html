About us

The ICEBERG Philosophy: Happiness is in science. Our name is at the heart of everything we do. We know that the pressure of academic deadlines and exam results are just the tip—the small part that everyone sees. But the real prize—a solid academic foundation that determines future success, lies just beneath the surface. That foundation is built with confidence, resilience, effective study habits, and a true passion for learning. These are the qualities we instill in our students. The mission at ICEBERG is to build the strong, solid base that will support our students in all their future academic journeys and beyond.
