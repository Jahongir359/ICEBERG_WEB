About ICEBERG Study Center

ICEBERG Study Center is an education center based in Namangan, Uzbekistan. We provide English language education, IELTS and SAT preparation, academic tutoring, and student mentoring programs designed to help learners build strong academic foundations and reach their goals.

ICEBERG Study Center — Namangan, O'zbekistonda faoliyat yurituvchi ta'lim markazi. Markaz ingliz tili, IELTS va SAT tayyorgarligi, akademik repetitorlik hamda o'quvchilar uchun mentorlik dasturlarini taklif qiladi.
