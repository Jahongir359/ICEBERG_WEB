About us

The ICEBERG Philosophy: Happiness is in science !!! Our name is at the heart of everything we do. We see a student's potential as an iceberg. The grades and exam results are just the tip—the small part that everyone sees. But the real power, the massive, unseen foundation that determines future success, lies just beneath the surface. That foundation is built from confidence, resilience, effective study habits, and a true passion for learning. While other centers may focus only on the tip, our mission at ICEBERG is to build the strong, solid base that will support your child through their entire academic journey and beyond.
