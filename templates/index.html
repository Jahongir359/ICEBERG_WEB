Grammar has always been my weak point. Comma splices, misplaced modifiers, you name it, I did it. With the SATs coming up, I knew I had to get serious about improving my writing mechanics. After a few months of weekly sessions, I took the SAT and my score on the Evidence-Based Reading and Writing section was 120 points higher than my first practice test! My writing in school assignments is much cleaner now, too. Iceberg gave me the specific tools and confidence I needed to succeed.
